# Viewer Engagement & Strategic Content Investment Analysis — StreamWave Entertainment Platform
Project Title: Viewer Engagement & Strategic Content Investment Analysis — StreamWave Entertainment Platform
Role: Data Analyst
Tools & Skills: Microsoft Excel (dashboards, pivot tables, KPI tracking), Data Visualization, PowerPoint (executive presentation), Business Report Writing, Strategic Analysis

Project Overview
StreamWave is a fictional streaming entertainment platform facing intensifying competition, rising content costs, and growing subscriber churn. As the lead data analyst on this project, I was tasked with analyzing viewer activity data across 999 users to uncover engagement patterns, identify high and low-performing content genres, and deliver a data-driven content investment strategy to platform executives.

What I Did
I built and analyzed an interactive Excel dashboard tracking key metrics including active user rates, genre popularity, subscription tier distribution, concurrent viewing behavior, and monthly subscriber acquisition versus cancellation trends. From this, I produced a full executive reporting suite consisting of a 10-slide PowerPoint presentation and a professional presenter script.
The analysis covered five core dimensions: audience demographics, genre performance, subscription trends, content engagement depth, and genre engagement quality scoring.

Key Findings

Drama (22.1%) and Comedy (21.4%) together accounted for nearly 44% of all platform views, making them the clear anchors of the content library
Cancellations (141 annually) outpaced new subscriber acquisitions (51) by a factor of 2.76×, with a critical churn spike concentrated in June through August
Horror, Musical, and Thriller genres combined for less than 0.7% of total viewership, representing near-zero return on content investment
36% of users were repeat viewers, with an average of 60 content titles consumed per user — signaling strong library depth engagement
45% of users remained on the Basic subscription tier, representing a significant untapped revenue conversion opportunity


Recommendations Delivered
I translated the findings into six strategic recommendations: prioritizing Drama and Comedy acquisitions, launching a proactive mid-year retention campaign, investing in Sci-Fi and Documentary originals, divesting from underperforming genres, converting Basic-tier users to Premium, and building a loyalty program targeting the repeat viewer segment.
